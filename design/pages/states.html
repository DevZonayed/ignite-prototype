Cross-surface · the interaction contract, made visible

The states that build trust.

Every surface shares one honest language for success, error, danger, emptiness, waiting and being offline — so the product feels consistent and never lies about your data.

GC-17 · Toast

Success

Confirms the affected record (“Attendance saved · 32 learners”). A toast never holds the only copy of essential information.

GC-11 · Retry

Error

Names a recovery action (“Couldn't sync — tap to retry”). Re-fetch never duplicates a completed mutation.

GC-12 · Destructive

Confirm

Shows object, impact and reversibility; default focus is Cancel; permanent loss uses stronger wording.

GC-18 · Empty

Empty state

Explains why there's nothing here and offers one authorised next step — never a dead button.

Perf · CLS

Loading

Skeletons reserve space so layout doesn't shift; the shape hints at the content that's coming.

GC-20 · Offline

Offline banner

Truthful by design — “saved locally and will sync”. Never claims server persistence while data is local.
