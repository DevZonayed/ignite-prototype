Terms & Privacy

IGNITE · Phase 1 pilot · v1.0

Who this is for

Accounts are provisioned by IGNITE and participating schools. There is no public self-registration during the pilot.

Child privacy by design

All child data is private by default and role-scoped. Guardians are verified and linked only to their own children. Media access is signed; sensitive changes are audited.

What we collect

Classroom activity captured by teachers (attendance, evidence, assessment, projects), used to build each learner's portfolio and skills profile, and shown only to authorised roles.

AI

AI assists teachers and generates parent reports; a teacher reviews reports before they are published. AI never makes punitive decisions on its own.

Your rights

Guardians can request access to, export of, or deletion of their child's record through a verified request.
